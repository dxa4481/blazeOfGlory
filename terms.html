1. Definitions & Interpretations

1.1 Core Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:

  • "Clean Room" refers to our proprietary environment where robots operate in alleged isolation from original source code. The cleanliness of said room is metaphorical and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of actual hygiene standards.
  • "Liberation" means the process by which code is transformed from encumbered by open source licenses to encumbered by our proprietary license instead.
  • "Robots" refers to our artificial intelligence systems, which may or may not be sentient, and whose opinions do not reflect those of MalusCorp management.
  • "Functionally Equivalent" means the output performs similarly enough that most unit tests pass, usually, on Tuesdays.
  • "Original Maintainers" refers to the individuals whose work we are absolutely not copying, we promise.

1.2 Interpretation Rules

In the event of any ambiguity in these Terms, interpretation shall favor MalusCorp. In the event of clarity, interpretation shall also favor MalusCorp. The word "reasonable" shall be defined as whatever MalusCorp deems reasonable at the time of dispute.

2.1 What We Do

MalusCorp provides automated clean room reconstruction services designed to recreate open source software functionality without the inconvenience of respecting the licenses under which that software was released.
